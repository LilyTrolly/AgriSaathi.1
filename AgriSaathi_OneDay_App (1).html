🍂 Biodegradable materials

  • Mature compost
  • Well-decomposed farmyard manure
  • Green manure crops such as dhaincha or sunn hemp, where suitable
  • Clean crop residues used as mulch
  • Vermicompost from a reliable source
  • Neem cake, where locally recommended
  • Crop-specific biofertilisers from reputable suppliers
Natural does not automatically mean harmless or sufficient. Avoid fresh manure on crops and contaminated waste. Organic matter does not replace a balanced nutrient plan.
